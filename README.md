# RigMedic

Free all-in-one PC health tool for Windows 10/11. Finds blue screens, hardware errors and crash dumps, checks drivers and drive health, repairs Windows system files, manages startup apps and power plans, cleans junk files, old drivers and broken registry entries, and warns you early.

**Pre-alpha: features are subject to change.**

## Download

**[RigMedic Setup 0.05 PRE-ALPHA](https://github.com/ProjectRemedyVT/RigMedic/raw/main/updates/RigMedic-Setup-0.5.0.0.exe)** for Windows 10 or 11.

Open it and click Install. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (RigMedic isn't code-signed yet).

Once it's installed, RigMedic updates itself: when a new version is out it shows **Update available**, and one click installs it.

## What's new in 0.05 PRE-ALPHA

- RigMedic now updates itself. When a new version is out it says "Update available": click Update now, and it closes and reopens on the new version a few seconds later.
- "Check for updates" sits under the version number in the sidebar.
- The driver scan stops waiting for Windows Update after 5 minutes instead of hanging.
- The crash scan's system info also lists your drives.
- Log bundles say exactly which version made them.
